THE PROBLEM

When the user can't ask for help.

Falls, accidents, sudden health distress, extreme heat, floods and other emergencies can happen when a person is alone or unable to access a phone. RESQ ONE is designed to reduce that dependence on manual SOS action.

◉ SENSE

Collect signals from motion, pulse/wellness and the surrounding environment.

✦ THINK

Process signals locally using filtering and sensor-fusion logic.

! DECIDE

Identify potential falls, impacts, abnormal wellness or environmental events.

⌁ ALERT

Provide SOS, location and emergency communication through available connectivity.

OUR DIFFERENTIATOR

One device. Multiple ways to wear.

Instead of designing the intelligence around only one wearable form, RESQ ONE is envisioned as a modular core that can adapt to different situations.

⌚
Watch

Everyday use and quick emergency access.

◉
Necklace

◒

▣

Attach to clothing, belt, bag or equipment.

Privacy-first architecture

RESQ ONE is designed to perform relevant processing locally wherever practical, reducing unnecessary dependence on cloud processing.

FEASIBILITY

Build it in stages. Validate it properly.

RESQ ONE is currently at the concept and design stage. The physical functional MVP is planned as the next engineering phase.

PHASE 1

Architecture and component selection.

PHASE 2

MVP sensors, MCU, fall detection, SOS and GPS/BLE.

PHASE 3

Compact PCB, enclosure and wearable attachments.

PHASE 4

Testing, false-alert reduction and battery optimisation.

PHASE 5

Cellular/GNSS, environmental intelligence and advanced AI.
